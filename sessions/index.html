Explore Sessions are one-to-one conversations with HEL for the things that do not arrive neatly packaged.

You do not need to know exactly what the problem is.

You do not need to arrive with a goal.

You do not need to have the right words.

We begin with what is here.

YOU MIGHT BRING
  • a life transition
  • a decision you keep circling
  • a pattern you are beginning to notice
  • a relationship or dynamic you want to understand differently
  • a creative idea that has not yet found form
  • a feeling that something in your life is changing
  • a project, possibility or direction you cannot yet see clearly
  • a part of yourself that seems to be asking for more room
  • a situation where your usual way of thinking is no longer enough
WHAT HAPPENS

We pay attention.

To what is said.
To what keeps repeating.
To what does not fit.
To what is emerging underneath the obvious story.

We follow the threads, test assumptions, notice patterns, make connections and give shape to what may have been felt but not yet seen clearly.

There is no fixed formula.

Sometimes the session brings a decision.

Sometimes language.

Sometimes a new question.

Sometimes a map.

Sometimes the recognition that something is already changing.

THE SESSION

60 minutes · online · one-to-one

This is not about forcing an outcome.

It is about seeing more clearly what is actually here.

And from there, what wants to move.
