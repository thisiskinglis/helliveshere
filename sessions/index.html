HOW WE WORK

We pay attention. To what is said. To what keeps repeating. To what does not fit. To what is felt but not yet articulated.

There is no fixed formula and no checklist that decides who you are before we begin.

Sometimes we work through language — finding precise words for something that has remained vague.
Sometimes through structure — seeing the pattern, relationship, system or loop you are inside.
Sometimes we stay with what has been avoided, minimised, protected or difficult to reconcile.

Psychology is the ground. Systems thinking is the lens. Your lived experience is the material.

HEL sessions are a psychology-led process of inquiry, sense-making and translation.

It is to understand more clearly what is happening, what may be shaping it, and why it has been difficult to resolve.
