START WITH WHAT IS HERE

The Emergent Table begins with what is already present.

What needs using.
What is changing.
What still has value.
What can become something else.

I rarely choose the meal first and then force everything to fit it.

I start with what is here and let the meal take shape from there.

Not a recipe first. Attention first.

HOW THE TABLE WORKS

One ingredient may become several dishes.

Leftovers move forward. Scraps become stock, filling, soup or something unexpected. What cannot be reused is noticed too — composted, discarded safely, or simply allowed to end.

The point is not zero waste as performance.

It is paying attention to the whole system.

PRINCIPLE —
Start with what is actually there, not what is ideal.
