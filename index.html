Not “hell.” — HEL

HEL is a psychology-informed practice of deep listening, pattern recognition and creative translation.

At the centre of HEL are private 1:1 Translation Sessions. You bring something you are trying to understand, a recurring pattern, conflicting feelings, a transition, a decision, an identity that no longer fits, or simply something you can feel but cannot quite put into words.

I listen for what sits underneath what you are saying: the patterns, contradictions, repetitions, connections and tensions that can be difficult to see when you are inside them. Then I translate what we uncover into a form you can return to — such as a written piece, personal map, visual, voice piece or another form that fits.

Writing and sound are other expressions of the same practice.

HOW HEL TAKES FORM.

Sessions. Writing. Sound.

HEL gives form to what is hidden, contradictory or difficult to name — so it can be seen, understood, held or felt.

01 — SESSIONS

You bring what won't settle. I translate it.

A private 1:1 Translation Session grounded in psychological inquiry.

You might bring a decision you cannot resolve, conflicting feelings, a relationship or experience you cannot quite make sense of, a transition, an identity that no longer fits, or simply the sense that something is happening beneath the surface that you cannot yet articulate.

Through focused conversation, questioning and interpretation, I work with what you bring — the emotional tensions, contradictions, meanings, assumptions, omissions and recurring themes within it.

The aim is to arrive at a clearer understanding of what is actually happening beneath the surface.

After the session, I take that understanding further and translate it into something you can return to:

written reflection / personal map / psychological narrative / manifesto / monologue / visual translation / audio / another form that fits

The form is not decided in advance. It follows what we actually uncover.

TRANSLATION SESSION — 1:1

You bring what won't settle. I translate it.

You do not need to arrive with a clear question or the right words.

Bring the decision you keep circling. The pattern that keeps repeating. The conflicting feelings that both seem true. The identity that no longer quite fits. The experience you understand intellectually but still cannot make sense of.

We start with whatever is actually there.

You speak. I ask questions, follow connections and track the structure within what you bring.

I PAY ATTENTION TO —

  • what keeps repeating—
  • where different parts of your story contradict each other—
  • what carries more weight than it first appears to—
  • what is absent, avoided or difficult to articulate—
  • the language and ideas you keep returning to—
  • connections between things that may initially seem unrelated—
  • what you already sense but have not yet been able to put into words—

ABOUT KAYLEE-JANE

The human source.

HEL comes from Kaylee-Jane Inglis — a psychologist, systems thinker and lifelong student of human behaviour, identity and consciousness.

My interest has always been in what sits beneath the obvious: how people make meaning, how identity shifts, how patterns form, and what happens when someone reaches a point where the way they have understood themselves no longer quite fits.

Psychology gave me one way of understanding people. Systems thinking taught me to look for patterns, relationships and structure.

This I use both without reducing a person to a diagnosis, framework or neat explanation.

HEL brings psychological depth into a different kind of encounter — one that explores what is happening beneath the surface, makes sense of what is difficult to articulate, and gives that understanding a form you can return to.
