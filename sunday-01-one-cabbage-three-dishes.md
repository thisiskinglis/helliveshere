# FIELD NOTE // 01: One cabbage, three dishes, two soups, almost nothing wasted

> Sunday cook with me. No recipe. Start with what is actually there.

This week the fridge said: one cabbage that needed using. So we started there.

### 1. Cabbage Rolls
**Ingredients:**
- cabbage leaves, leftover rice, onion, spices
**Method:**
1. Blanch leaves, roll with filling, bake with tomato.

### 2. Pumpkin Bread
**Ingredients:**
- pumpkin puree, flour, butter, yeast
**Method:**
1. Mix dough in pink bowl, prove, bake - sliced and buttered warm.

### 3. Cauliflower Curry + Two Soups
**Ingredients:**
- cauliflower, curry spices, broth from scraps
**Method:**
1. Curry for tonight, soups from stems and outer leaves for tomorrow.

### What emerged?
No shopping. Just what needed to be used. Same as HEL - we don't start with the ideal, we start with what is actually there. Permissionless emergence.
